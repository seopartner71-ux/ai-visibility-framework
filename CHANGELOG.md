# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-10

### Added
- Initial public release of AI Visibility Framework v1.0.
- Defined the AI Visibility concept as a practical methodology for assessing and improving brand visibility in generative AI search systems.
- Added the six-layer framework architecture: Entity Authority, Semantic Coverage, Content Quality, Source Trust, Citation Presence, and Structured Knowledge.
- Introduced the AI Visibility Score v1.0 model with a 0–100 scale and working weights.
- Added the AI Visibility Maturity Model with five levels from Invisible to Advanced AI Visibility.
- Added the AI Visibility Audit methodology as the operational layer of the framework.
- Added the Query Set Methodology for AI citation analysis.
- Added the Relationship with SEO section to clarify the role of AI Visibility alongside traditional SEO.
- Added limitations, future research notes, and an author statement.

### Changed
- Reframed the methodology as an assessment model based on observable external signals rather than internal AI system logic.
- Replaced absolute visibility claims with diagnostic and comparative evaluation language.
- Standardized the project structure for public research use on GitHub.
