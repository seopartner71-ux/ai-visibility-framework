# AI Visibility Framework v1.0

**A practical methodology for measuring and improving brand visibility in generative AI search systems**

**Author:** Vladimir Sinitsyn  
**Organization:** Systemnoe SEO  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Release date:** August 2026

---

## Overview

AI Visibility Framework v1.0 is an authorial practical methodology for assessing and developing brand visibility in generative search.

The framework is designed to work systematically with a new search environment where the user receives not only a list of links, but ready-made answers, recommendations and explanations from AI systems.

The methodology combines existing directions:

- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Entity SEO
- Semantic Search
- Content Strategy
- Digital PR
- Structured Data
- Generative AI Search Optimization

The main objective of the Framework is to help companies understand:

- how well AI systems understand their brand;
- which sources form the perception of the company;
- what trust signals exist;
- what actions can improve AI Visibility.

**Important:**  
AI Visibility Framework does not claim to reveal the internal algorithms of ChatGPT, Gemini, Yandex Neuro or other AI systems.  
The framework is based on analysis of observable external signals related to the representation of digital entities in generative search.

---

## Why AI Visibility Matters

Traditional search model was built around:

```text
Query → Page → Position → Click → Traffic
```

Generative search changes the user journey:

```text
Query → AI understands the task → Analyzes sources → Forms answer → 
Selects most relevant entities → Recommends information to the user
```

In this environment, it is not enough to just occupy positions.  
A brand must:

- exist as a clear digital entity;
- have confirmed expertise;
- be associated with specific topics;
- be present in sources used by AI systems.

---

## What is AI Visibility

AI Visibility is the degree to which a brand is represented, recognized and used by generative AI systems when forming responses to users.

AI Visibility includes:

- Entity Recognition
- Semantic Association
- Source Representation
- Citation Presence

### AI Visibility vs Traditional SEO

AI Visibility differs from traditional search visibility.

**Traditional SEO focuses on:**

- positions
- impressions
- clicks
- organic traffic

**AI Visibility focuses on:**

- understanding of the brand entity
- thematic connections
- source trust
- citation
- presence inside AI answers

SEO answers the question:  
**“Where is the page?”**

AI Visibility answers the question:  
**“How well does AI understand, trust and use information about the brand?”**

## Framework Architecture

The framework consists of six main components.

### Layer 1 — Entity Authority

Goal: Assess how clearly the brand’s digital entity is formed.

Checked:

- consistent information about the company
- connection between brand and experts
- consistency of data across sources
- organization and author pages
- Knowledge Graph signals

### Layer 2 — Semantic Coverage

Goal: Assess the thematic depth of the brand.

Checked:

- topics associated with the brand
- how fully the niche is covered
- presence of thematic clusters
- coverage of user questions

### Layer 3 — Content Quality

Goal: Assess the availability of evidence of expertise.

Checked:

- research
- cases
- expert materials
- original data
- practical experience

AI systems need not only texts, but proof of competence.

### Layer 4 — Source Trust

Goal: Assess external trust in the brand.

Checked:

- publications
- media
- expert profiles
- links
- independent mentions

### Layer 5 — Citation Presence

Goal: Assess the actual presence of the brand in AI answers.

Checked:

- commercial queries
- expert queries
- informational queries

Analyzed:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Yandex Neuro
- other AI Search systems

Evaluated:

- whether the brand appears
- which sources are used
- which competitors are presented more often

### Layer 6 — Structured Knowledge

Goal: Assess the technical readiness of digital data.

Checked:

- Schema.org
- JSON-LD
- Organization
- Person
- Article
- SameAs
- Sitemap
- indexing

## AI Visibility Score v1.0

AI Visibility Score is an evaluation model showing the brand’s readiness for generative search.

**Overall score:** 0–100 points

| Component            | Weight |
|----------------------|--------|
| Entity Authority     | 20     |
| Semantic Coverage    | 20     |
| Content Quality      | 20     |
| Source Trust         | 15     |
| Citation Presence    | 15     |
| Structured Knowledge | 10     |
| **Total**            | **100**|

**Weighting Model Note:**  
The weighting coefficients of AI Visibility Score v1.0 are working assumptions based on current observations and practical experience.  
In future versions, the model may be adjusted based on:

- new data
- industry research
- practical cases
- additional observations of AI Search systems

**Important:**  
AI Visibility Score is not a forecast of the brand’s inclusion in AI responses.  
It is:

- an assessment model
- an evaluation framework
- a practical methodology

## AI Visibility Maturity Model

**Level 0 — Invisible (0–20)**  
The brand is virtually absent in the AI environment.

Signs:

- AI poorly understands the entity
- few sources
- no structured information

**Level 1 — Recognized (21–40)**  
The brand exists online, but the connection between:

- company
- experts
- topic

is weak.

**Level 2 — Established (41–60)**  
There is:

- content base
- thematic connection
- basic trust signals

**Level 3 — Trusted (61–80)**  
There are:

- external confirmations
- expert materials
- stable presence in sources

**Level 4 — AI Visible (81–100)**  
The brand has:

- a strong digital entity
- high thematic coherence
- stable presence in AI responses

## AI Visibility Audit v1.0

The framework is applied through a practical audit.

**AI Visibility Audit includes:**

1. **Entity Analysis**  
   - whether AI understands the brand  
   - whether a single entity exists  
   - whether the company and experts are linked

2. **Semantic Analysis**  
   - thematic connections  
   - content gaps  
   - user intents

3. **Content Analysis**  
   - expert materials  
   - research  
   - cases  
   - original data

4. **Trust Analysis**  
   - external publications  
   - mentions  
   - expert profiles

5. **AI Citation Analysis**  
   - AI responses  
   - recommendations  
   - citation sources

6. **Technical AI Readiness**  
   - Schema  
   - structured data  
   - indexing  
   - technical accessibility of information

## Limitations

AI Visibility Framework has limitations.

The methodology:

- does not reveal the internal algorithms of AI systems
- does not guarantee the appearance of the brand in AI responses
- is not a replacement for traditional SEO
- evolves along with changes in AI Search

Version 1.0 is the first iteration of the model and will be improved through practical application and new research.
